The Bot Roster

Random Bot
EASY

No logic, no memory. Plays Rock, Paper, or Scissors at pure random. The baseline — even humans should beat it consistently over many rounds.

Easy 1
EASY

Cycles a fixed sequence: R → R → P → P → S → repeat. Entirely predictable once the pattern is spotted — usually within 5 rounds.

Easy 2
EASY

Scans your last 10 moves, finds the most frequent choice, and plays its counter. Exploit it by varying your moves evenly.

Medium 1
MEDIUM

Reads your last move and immediately counters it. Simple but reactive. Beating it requires thinking two steps ahead — play what beats its counter.

Medium 2
MEDIUM

Builds a 2nd-order Markov table: tracks which move follows each pair of consecutive moves. Predicts based on your two-move sequences.

Markov Chain
HARD

The apex predator. Builds transition probability tables from sequences up to length 5. Predicts your next move with confidence scoring — and gets sharper the longer you play.
